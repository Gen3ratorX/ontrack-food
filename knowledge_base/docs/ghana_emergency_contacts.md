# Emergency numbers in Ghana

- 112 — national emergency number; the National Ambulance Service promotes 112 as its 24/7 emergency hotline for medical emergencies nationwide.
- 193 — Ambulance Service (listed by the Ministry of the Interior).
- 191 or 18555 — Ghana Police Service. 192 — Ghana National Fire Service.

When to use: a person with diabetes who is unconscious, having a seizure, confused and unable to swallow, breathing fast and deep with fruity breath, vomiting and unable to keep fluids down, or showing signs of stroke or heart attack needs emergency help — call 112 or 193, or go to the nearest hospital emergency unit.
