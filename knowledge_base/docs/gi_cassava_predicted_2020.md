# Predicted GI of cassava foods consumed in Ghana (laboratory estimate)

IMPORTANT: these are predicted GI values from laboratory (in-vitro) starch digestion, not measured in people. Do not mix them with in-vivo GI values without saying so.

| Food | Predicted GI (%) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Raw cassava | 47.75 | J Food Nutr Sci 2020 |
| Boiled cassava (ampesi) | 77.30 | J Food Nutr Sci 2020 |
| Akyeke | 79.05 | J Food Nutr Sci 2020 |
| Kokonte, from sun-dried cassava flour | 40.20 | J Food Nutr Sci 2020 |
| Kokonte, from solar-dried cassava flour | 61.11 | J Food Nutr Sci 2020 |

Authors' findings: steaming and boiling increased predicted GI; fermentation had no significant effect on predicted GI of fermented steamed products; total starch and amylopectin raise GI while amylose and dietary fibre lower it.
