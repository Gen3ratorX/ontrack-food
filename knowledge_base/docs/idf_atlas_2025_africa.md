# Diabetes in the IDF Africa region (IDF Diabetes Atlas, 11th edition)

- In 2024 the IDF Africa (AFR) region had an estimated 24.59 million adults aged 20–79 with diabetes (95% UI 21.92–27.27 million), projected to reach 59.53 million by 2050 — a 142% relative increase, the highest of any IDF region.
- Age-standardised prevalence in AFR was 5.04% in 2024, the lowest of the IDF regions, projected to be 5.87% in 2050.
- IDF Atlas 2025 estimates that 72.6% of adults with diabetes in the Africa region were undiagnosed in 2024, the highest proportion of any region (IDF Diabetes Atlas 11th ed., chapter 3/4; NCBI Bookshelf NBK618744).
- Ghana-specific figures: not included here because sources found give conflicting numbers (to be verified against the Atlas country table, Table S7, before use).

Implication for OnTrack: many people may have undiagnosed diabetes. OnTrack must not diagnose; it should encourage testing at a health facility when someone describes symptoms such as excessive thirst, frequent urination, blurred vision or unexplained weight loss.
